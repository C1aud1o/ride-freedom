# Deleting your Spindro account

*Last updated: [DATE]*

> This page must be published on the website at a public, stable address: Google Play requires it in the app's store listing. Planned address: [ACCOUNT DELETION PAGE URL].

You can delete your Spindro account whenever you want. It is free.

Spindro also works **without an account**. If you never created one, we hold no data about you: everything is on your device (see below).

## How to delete the account from the app

1. Open Spindro and sign in to your account.
2. Go to **Settings → Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Deletion is permanent. It cannot be undone.

## How to delete the account by email

If you cannot sign in to the app or you already uninstalled it:

1. Write to **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.
2. Use the subject **"Spindro account deletion"**.
3. Write from the **email address of the account**, or tell us which Google account you sign in with. This lets us check the account is yours. We never ask for your password.

We delete the account and confirm by email **within one month** of the request (the maximum time set by the GDPR). Usually much sooner.

## What is deleted

- your profile (email or Google account link, user identifier, display name, Country);
- synced data: ride history, records, custom workouts, settings;
- your **heart rate**, if you had consented to storing it on the server;
- your leaderboard scores and challenge medals.

## What remains

- **On your device:** local data is not touched by deleting the account. To remove it, clear the site data in your browser, or on Android go to *Settings → Apps → Spindro → Storage → Clear data*, or uninstall the app.
- **On Strava:** activities already uploaded to Strava stay on Strava. You manage them from your Strava account, which is not ours. To remove the connection use "Disconnect" in the app, or revoke Spindro in Strava's settings (*Settings → My Apps*).
- **Technical copies:** our provider's systems (Google/Firebase) may keep technical safety copies for a short time before final removal.
- **Legal obligations:** if the law requires us to keep some data, we keep it only for that reason.

## Deleting only part of the data

If you want to delete only something, not the account:

- **Heart rate on the server:** turn off the option "Also save heart rate in the cloud" in the settings. We delete the heart rate already stored.
- **Other data:** write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and tell us what you want removed.
