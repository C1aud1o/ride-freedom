## Rules for us (the people who maintain Spindro)

1. **Text only.** We mention compatibility with the name in plain text, in the same font as the rest of the page. We do not put third-party logos, icons or artwork in the app, the website, store images or videos.
2. **Strava exception.** For Strava we use, where needed, the official **"Compatible with Strava"** badge (and the official "Connect with Strava" button), downloaded from Strava's site, **unmodified**, at the sizes and under the conditions in the [Strava brand guidelines](https://developers.strava.com/guidelines/). The guidelines are rechecked before each new use.
3. **No confusion.** We do not use third-party names as part of the app's name or as the developer's name. We never say or imply that a company endorses, recommends or works with us.
4. **Facts only.** We write "compatible with" only where we verified it or where the Bluetooth protocol guarantees it, and we say so. We do not promise that Spindro works with every model. The list of compatible trainers also relies on user reports.
5. **No unfair comparisons.** In promotion we may say Spindro is a free alternative, but we do not disparage other products or put words in third parties' mouths.
6. **Standard sentence.** Wherever we mention trademarks (store listing, website, "Compatible trainers" page, README) we add: *"Zwift, Garmin, Strava, Tacx, Wahoo, Van Rysel, Decathlon and YouTube are trademarks of their respective owners. Spindro is not affiliated with these companies."*
7. **YouTube.** We use the official player and do not hide or change its notices. We do not use the YouTube logo.
8. **Strava data.** We do not use data read from Strava for leaderboards or to show to other users (Strava API rules).
9. **If an owner writes to us** asking to change or remove a mention, we do it and reply.
