## 4. Safety: train safely

Physical activity carries risks.

- **See a doctor** before starting or increasing training, especially if you have heart, blood pressure or other conditions, if you are sedentary or if you are returning after a break. This applies in particular to maximum-effort tests, such as the FTP test, and to sprints.
- Spindro is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Heart rate zones and goals are general guidance.
- **Stop** if you feel pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath or discomfort.
- **Set up your trainer safely**: bike firmly mounted, stable surface, ventilation, water within reach.
- **Games must not distract you from safety.** Looking at the screen while you pedal can make you lose balance or trip. Do not play in a way that makes you forget how your body feels or where you are. Do not use Spindro off the trainer, while riding on the road or while driving any vehicle.
- Anyone training with children or teenagers must supervise them.

You use Spindro and train **at your own responsibility**.
